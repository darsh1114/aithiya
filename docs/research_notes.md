# Cultural Pilot Research Notes

## Officially reviewed sources

| Record | Source | Verified pilot metadata |
|---|---|---|
| Kumbh Mela | [UNESCO ICH](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/kumbh-mela-01258) | UNESCO describes a rotating sacred-river pilgrimage held at Prayagraj, Haridwar, Ujjain, and Nashik; the record will use the multi-city location model and winter/spring seasonality. |
| Durga Puja in Kolkata | [UNESCO ICH](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/durga-puja-in-kolkata-00703) | UNESCO identifies Kolkata, West Bengal; the annual observance occurs in September or October and centers on ten days of worship, art, and public installations. |
| Pongal | [Utsav, Ministry of Tourism](https://utsav.gov.in/public/event-category/harvest-festival) | The Ministry of Tourism's Utsav page describes a four-day January Tamil Nadu harvest observance honouring nature, rain, the Sun, cattle, and communal food. |
| Hornbill Festival | [Utsav, Ministry of Tourism](https://utsav.gov.in/public/view-event/hornbill-festival) | The Utsav event record identifies Kisama Heritage Village near Kohima, Nagaland, and describes a ten-day annual State Government event showcasing Naga cultural heritage, including performances, crafts, indigenous games, and cuisine. |
| Thalassery Biriyani | [Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/kerala/thiruvananthapuram/what-to-eat-and-where-to-eat-in-thiruvananthapuram) | The Ministry of Tourism destination page identifies the dish with Kerala's Malabar coast and describes its fragrant basmati rice, meat, spices, fried onions, cashews, and raisins. |
| Appam | [Incredible India](https://prod.incredibleindia.gov.in/content/incredible-india-v2/en/destinations/kochi/food-and-cuisine.html) | The official Kochi food page describes appam as a fermented rice-batter and coconut-milk preparation commonly eaten for breakfast or dinner. |

## Data-quality rule

Every approved pilot record must retain a valid item-level source URL, a source-confidence value assigned per record, an explicit location, and season-month metadata. Candidate-only entries are excluded from the public import.

## UNESCO core selection

UNESCO's [India register](https://ich.unesco.org/en/state/india-IN) lists 16 inscribed elements as of 2025, including Deepavali, Nawrouz, Garba of Gujarat, Durga Puja in Kolkata, Kumbh Mela, Yoga, Thatheras' utensil craft, Sankirtana, Buddhist chanting of Ladakh, Chhau, Kalbelia, Mudiyettu, Ramman, Kutiyattam, Vedic chanting, and Ramlila. These form the authoritative base for the pilot's traditions, stories, and festivals.

The item-level [Yoga record](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/yoga-01163) confirms UNESCO inscription in 2016 and describes yoga as an intergenerational practice involving poses, meditation, controlled breathing, chanting, and traditional Guru-Shishya transmission. Its location has been recorded as New Delhi for discoverability, while its scope is national rather than city-limited.
